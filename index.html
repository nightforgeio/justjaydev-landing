It depends on pages and features. After the free audit you get a fixed quote, so there are no surprise bills. Small business sites are designed to be affordable.

A typical business site takes 1–2 weeks from approved preview to launch. Web apps with admin panels take longer, and you'll get a clear timeline upfront.
